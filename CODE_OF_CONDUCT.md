# Code of conduct

Medics Help is built for doctors, nurses, employers and everyone who helps them. We expect everyone who takes part in our repositories, issues and discussions to treat others with respect.

## Our standards

- Be respectful, patient and kind, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent and keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Do not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone, for any reason.
- Respect that many people here write in their second or third language.

## Protect personal and medical data

- **Never post real personal data or medical data** in issues, pull requests, commits or comments. This includes patient information, health records, names, contact details, ID or document numbers, diplomas and certificates.
- Use made-up examples instead. If you post real data by mistake, tell us at [info@medics.help](mailto:info@medics.help) and we will remove it.

## Not for emergencies or medical advice

Our repositories are not a place to get medical help. Medics Help gives no medical advice to patients.

**In an emergency, call your local emergency number. In the EU, call 112.**

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks this code of conduct, email [info@medics.help](mailto:info@medics.help). We will review every report, treat it confidentially and respond as quickly as we can. We may remove content or block people who break these rules.
