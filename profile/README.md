<h3 align="center">Your medical career, recognised in Europe.</h3>

Medics Help is an AI platform that guides doctors and nurses trained outside the EU back to practice in Europe. We help with recognition and paperwork, train medical language with a voice AI, prepare candidates for exams and introduce them to hospitals. It is free for doctors and nurses and paid for by employers.

> **Status: in development.** We are building the product now. Our first cohort starts in early 2027.

### What the AI does

- Plays patients, colleagues and examiners in practice conversations, by voice.
- Scores answers against the criteria examiners use, and explains every score.
- Answers questions about recognition using only official sources, and shows each source.

### What the AI does not do

- It never decides who gets hired. People at the hospital make that decision.
- It never gives medical advice to patients.
- It never replaces the official exam or the decision of the authority.

More in [responsible-ai](https://github.com/medicshelp/responsible-ai).
