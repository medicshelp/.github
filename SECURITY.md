# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security issue in anything published by Medics Help, including the website [medics.help](https://medics.help), please email **[info@medics.help](mailto:info@medics.help)** with the subject line **"Security"**.

Please include:

- what you found and where (URL, repository, file);
- steps to reproduce it;
- the possible impact, as far as you can tell.

**Please do not report vulnerabilities in public GitHub issues, pull requests or discussions**, and do not share them publicly before we have had a chance to fix them.

Do not access, change or delete data that does not belong to you, and do not run tests that could disrupt the service for others.

## What happens next

- We will confirm that we received your report.
- We will look into it, keep you informed about what we find and tell you when it is fixed.
- If you wish, we will credit you once the issue is resolved.

Thank you for helping keep Medics Help and the people who use it safe.
